Daymark Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

This policy covers Daymark: Plans & Reminders for Android and the Daymark website. Innocent Udechukwu operates Daymark and is responsible for its handling of personal data. For privacy questions and requests, email admin@nwachi.click.

Information Daymark uses

  • Account information. If you create an account, Daymark uses your email address and account identifier to sign you in, verify your account, and help you reset your password. Supabase Auth manages credentials and sessions.
  • Plans and sharing. Daymark stores the plans and reminder preferences you save, including titles, descriptions, dates, times and completion status. To support sharing, it uses the email address you enter to find the recipient and stores invitation and membership records. Invitees may see the sender’s email. People you give access to can see the shared plan. A group link can be forwarded and used by other verified users until the owner disables it.
  • Schedule imports. Daymark reads the photos or documents you select on your device to extract shift details. The original files stay on your device. The shift entries you review and choose to save sync to your Daymark account.
  • Reminders and device preferences. The Android app schedules local reminders from synced plans if you enable alerts. If you enable web alerts, Daymark stores a push subscription and basic delivery records. The browser stores your sign-in session and display preferences. Reminder notifications use generic text on the lock screen.
  • Support and technical information. If you contact Daymark, we receive your email and the information you send. Hosting, authentication and delivery providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request logs and delivery status to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Why we use information

We use account and plan information to provide the features you request, including sign-in, synchronisation, sharing and reminders. Where the GDPR applies, this processing is necessary to provide the service. We use limited technical and support information where necessary for our legitimate interests in operating and securing Daymark, and we handle requests or keep records when required by law. You choose whether to enable notifications and whether to share plans.

Who receives information

Supabase provides authentication and database hosting. Cloudflare serves the public Daymark website. Resend delivers transactional email such as verification and password-reset messages. Web push providers, including browser and device providers, deliver web reminders when enabled. Other Daymark users receive the plan or invitation information you choose to share with them. Daymark does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Daymark’s primary Supabase database is in Ireland. Providers may process some information elsewhere, for example to deliver messages, serve requests or provide support. Where required, applicable transfer safeguards include adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Contact Daymark for more information about those safeguards.

Security and retention

Daymark uses HTTPS and account-based access controls. Plans are accessible to their owner and to users with whom the owner shares access. The website’s offline cache stores interface files. It does not cache your account responses or plans.

We keep account details and saved plans while needed to provide the service, generally until you remove the data or delete your account. Scheduled web push delivery records are cleared after seven days. Providers may keep limited operational records or backup copies until their retention periods expire. We may retain limited information as needed for security, to resolve disputes or to meet a legal obligation.

Request account deletion

Email admin@nwachi.click with the subject Daymark account deletion. Include the email address on your Daymark account. You can send this request even after uninstalling the app. Never include your password or verification code.

We may verify that the account is yours before acting. Deletion removes the account and its associated active data, including your saved plans, memberships and notification subscriptions. Plans you own that you shared will also be removed. Limited records may remain for the reasons explained under Security and retention.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal information, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise these rights, contact admin@nwachi.click. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, such as the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

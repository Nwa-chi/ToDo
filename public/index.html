Daymark, one tap away

Add Daymark to your device for a focused app window and a home-screen icon.

  1. iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open in Chrome, then use its menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Windows or Mac: use the install icon in Chrome or Edge’s address bar. On Safari for Mac, choose File → Add to Dock.

Installation options depend on your browser. Internet is needed for your account and plans. Enable alerts after installing to receive push reminders.

Your information

Your email and plans are stored in the ToDo account service. Plans are private unless you invite one other verified user. Both people can edit and complete a shared plan. Only its owner can delete it. Your browser stores your login session and appearance preferences.

The offline cache contains only the app interface, not your plans or account responses. Sign out on shared devices. No advertising or analytics scripts are included.
